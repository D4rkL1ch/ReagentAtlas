Five tiers, in colours you already read

Every reagent gets a predicted value tier, coloured like item quality so you can judge it at a glance.

  1. VendorCopper Ore
  2. CommonPeacebloom, Mageroyal, dyes and threads
  3. UsefulLinen, Wool, Silk, Copper Bar
  4. ValuableIron Bar, Thick Leather, Runecloth
  5. Keep!Arcanite Bar, Black Lotus, Essences

The tier is a prediction, not a price. It weighs how many recipes use an item, how high their skill goes and what they lead to further down the crafting chain. With Auctionator installed, fresh auction prices move it up or down a step.

Everything else it shows you

All your alts at once
Recipes you know are green, recipes an alt knows are blue with their name. Owned counts add up bags and banks of every character, plus the warband bank.
Reagent browser
Type /ratlas to list every reagent in the game. Filter by profession, tier, what you own and what you can craft, and see where each recipe comes from.
Crafting tab
The other way round: search any recipe or crafted item to see what it takes, how many of each reagent you have, and who knows it or where to learn it.
Tracking list
Follow recipes and reagents in a small window. Set a goal and get a sound and a message when you have enough. The shopping list adds up what's still missing.
Loot alerts
Loot a recipe one of your characters could learn, or a Keep! reagent, and you hear about it. The same item stays quiet for 10 minutes while you farm.
Bag markers
A small tier-coloured pip on valuable items in your bags, so you can spot them without hovering. Works with ElvUI bags too.

Install

With the WagoApp, search for Reagent Atlas and click Install. To install by hand:

  1. Download the latest release from Wago or GitHub.
  2. Extract the ReagentAtlas folder into Interface\AddOns inside your WoW Forever folder.
  3. Start the game, or type /reload if it's already running.
  4. Log in on each character once, and open your bank and profession windows, so Reagent Atlas learns what they have and know.

You never have to type: every command is also a button, in the minimap menu or under Esc, Options, AddOns.

It only shows you things

Reagent Atlas never loots, sells, posts or scans the auction house for you. It reads your bags, banks and profession windows, and tells you what it knows.

Recipe data is built from the WoW Forever client. Where a recipe drops or who sells it comes partly from Classic-era data and isn't confirmed in Forever yet, and the addon says so when that's the case.

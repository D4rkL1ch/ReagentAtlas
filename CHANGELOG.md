# Reagent Atlas

## 0.3.1

- Fixed: a reagent shown on the world map with the Map button can now be taken off again. The button switches to Hide while its pins are shown; click it to remove them from the world map and minimap. Tracked reagents stay on the map as before.
- Changed: clicking a reagent in the browser no longer adds its pins to the map on its own; only the Map button does.

# Reagent Atlas

## 1.0.1

- **Fixed:** Lua errors in dungeons when holding Shift in combat, for example healing party members through their unit frames. After pressing Shift over an item tooltip, other parts of the game interface (raid frames, nameplates, the Settings panel) could also throw errors blaming Reagent Atlas for the rest of the session.
- Holding Shift still expands Reagent Atlas tooltips live on items in your bags and at merchants. Other item tooltips, such as links in chat, now show the Shift view the next time you hover them.

# Reagent Atlas

## 1.0.0

First full release, now also on CurseForge. No changes since 0.3.1; here is what Reagent Atlas does:

- **Item tooltips**: hover any item to see which recipes use it and the skill they need, which of your characters already know them, a value tier from Vendor to Keep!, and how many you own across all your characters' bags and banks.
- **Browser** (`/ratlas`): every reagent in the game, filtered by profession, value tier, owned or craftable. The Crafting tab shows what a recipe needs, how much of it you have, and who knows the recipe or where to learn it.
- **Bag markers**: optional pips on valuable items in your bags.
- **Tracking list** (`/ratlas track`): follow recipes and reagents, set goals and get a message and sound when you have enough. The shopping list adds up everything you still need.
- **Waylaid Crates**: the tooltip lists the bundles that fill a crate, cheapest first.
- **Loot map**: remembers where you looted each reagent and what dropped it, and shows tracked reagents as pins on the world map and minimap.
- **Auctionator** (optional): adds auction house prices to the value tiers.

Reagent Atlas only shows information. It never loots, sells, posts or scans for you.

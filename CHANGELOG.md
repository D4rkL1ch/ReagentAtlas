# Reagent Atlas

## 1.1.0

- **New:** herbalists and miners get a reminder when Find Herbs or Find Minerals is off: after logging in, after resurrecting, after leaving a dungeon, and shortly after the tracking is switched off. Never in combat or inside instances, and at most once a minute. Turn it off in Settings, or type `/ratlas tracking` to see your minimap tracking.
- **Updated:** recipe sources from the latest community data. About 170 more recipes now say where they drop or who sells them, and many dungeon recipes list every creature known to drop them.
- **Updated:** recipe data for the latest WoW Forever client. A few Tailoring bags need one more cloth, and one Leatherworking recipe can be learned earlier.
- **Fixed:** the loot map could name a creature after whatever you last cast a spell on, such as a party member you healed. Creatures are now only named from the corpse you loot.
- **Fixed:** the Defaults button in Settings now puts every option back to its default.
- **Fixed:** a profession briefly missing while the game loads no longer makes the addon forget its known recipes.
- **Fixed:** clicking Map in the browser during combat no longer marks the item as shown on the map.
- The Waylaid Crate tooltip now says why there are no auction prices: Auctionator is missing, turned off in Settings, or stopped after an error.
- Searching in the browser is faster.
